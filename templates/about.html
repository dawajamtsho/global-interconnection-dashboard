About The Project

Project background, development context, and purpose

The platform was developed under the collaborative initiative between Indian Institute of Technology Delhi and the International Solar Alliance to create a single, accessible interface for regional interconnection information. It is a cornerstone project executed by two M.Tech students from the Department of Energy Science and Engineering at IIT Delhi, currently pursuing M.Tech in Renewable Energy Technologies and Management under the Solar Fellowship program supported by the International Solar Alliance.

The platform integrates project datasets, policy intelligence, market references, and curated energy news into a unified web-based experience. It is designed to support researchers, planners, analysts, policymakers, and institutions engaged in regional power integration, cross-border electricity cooperation, and energy transition initiatives.

Why It Was Required

Cross-border electricity data is often fragmented across multiple sources. Decision-makers need infrastructure, market, policy, and news context together. Regional interconnection planning needs a faster way to compare countries and corridors. A shared dashboard improves visibility for collaboration across institutions and regions.

The project was required to reduce the gap between scattered data sources and practical decision-making needs. Cross-border electricity planning depends on understanding transmission assets, operating status, regional market structures, national and international policies, and the latest developments in the power sector.

By combining these layers in a single interface, the dashboard helps users move more quickly from information gathering to analysis, coordination, and action.
